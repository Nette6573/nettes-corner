20 Books You Should Read
in Your Twenties

By Nette
Jul 19, 2022
3 min read

Books, like any form of creative art, are life-changing. They have the power to influence how we think, act, and grow. Reading is one of the most powerful ways to develop yourself — not just mentally, but emotionally and spiritually as well.

Some of the benefits of reading include: reframing unhelpful thoughts, expanding your mindset, building motivation to pursue your goals, improving mental health, becoming more mindful, and increasing gratitude.

In this post, I'm sharing 20 books that I believe every young person should read. These recommendations are based on personal reading, reviews, and the powerful lessons each book teaches.

Finance & Mindset

1. Rich Dad Poor Dad – Robert Kiyosaki

A powerful introduction to financial literacy. This book challenges traditional beliefs about money and teaches the difference between working for money and having money work for you.

2. Think and Grow Rich – Napoleon Hill

A classic that explores how mindset and belief shape success. It emphasizes that your thoughts can either limit or elevate your life. A must-read for anyone serious about building something.

3. The Richest Man in Babylon – George S. Clason

Timeless financial wisdom told through parables set in ancient Babylon. Simple, practical principles that still apply today.

Leadership & Purpose

4. The 21 Irrefutable Laws of Leadership – John C. Maxwell

A guide to leadership that proves leadership is learned, not inherited, and can be developed by anyone willing to put in the work.

5. Start With Why – Simon Sinek

Explores why some people and organizations are more innovative and successful than others. It all starts with understanding your "why" — your core purpose and belief.

6. The Go-Giver – Bob Burg & John David Mann

This book teaches that success comes from giving value. It introduces five key laws centered around generosity, authenticity, and influence.

Change & Growth

7. Who Moved My Cheese – Spencer Johnson

A simple yet powerful story about change. It teaches how to adapt, let go of fear, and embrace new opportunities — even when the ground shifts beneath you.

8. Atomic Habits – James Clear

Perhaps the most practical book on behavior change ever written. Clear explains how tiny changes compound into remarkable results — and how your identity shapes your habits.

9. The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F*ck – Mark Manson

A counterintuitive guide to living a good life. Manson argues that improving our lives hinges on choosing what to care about and what to let go of.

"A book is a gift you can open again and again — and the right book at the right moment can change the entire trajectory of your life."

Self-Awareness & Faith

10. The Purpose Driven Life – Rick Warren

A deeply thoughtful exploration of why you exist and what you were made for. Whether you are deeply religious or simply searching for meaning, this book speaks to something universal.

11. You Are a Badass – Jen Sincero

A no-nonsense guide to identifying and changing self-sabotaging beliefs and behaviors so you can live a life you totally love.

12. The Power of Now – Eckhart Tolle

A guide to enlightenment that teaches the importance of living fully in the present moment, rather than being consumed by the past or future.

Relationships & People

13. How to Win Friends and Influence People – Dale Carnegie

A timeless classic on human relations. Carnegie's principles on how to genuinely connect with others remain as relevant today as when first published.

14. The 5 Love Languages – Gary Chapman

Explores the five ways people express and receive love. Understanding your love language — and others' — transforms every relationship in your life.

15. Attached – Amir Levine & Rachel Heller

A fascinating exploration of adult attachment theory and how your attachment style shapes every romantic relationship you have.

Five More Worth Your Time

16. The Alchemist – Paulo Coelho

A timeless story about following your dreams and listening to your heart. Beautiful, simple, and deeply moving.

17. Can't Hurt Me – David Goggins

A raw, powerful memoir about mastering your mind and pushing past what you think are your limits. Not for the faint-hearted.

18. Girl, Wash Your Face – Rachel Hollis

A call to stop believing the lies we tell ourselves about who we are and what we deserve.

19. Man's Search for Meaning – Viktor Frankl

One of the most profound books ever written. Frankl's account of surviving the Holocaust and his development of logotherapy will change how you see suffering and purpose.

20. The Four Agreements – Don Miguel Ruiz

A practical guide to personal freedom, offering four simple agreements that can transform your life if you truly commit to them.

You don't have to read them all at once. Start with the one that speaks to where you are right now. One book. One chapter. One page. Sometimes that's all it takes to shift something inside you.

Happy reading. — Nette
